Try it

Three trips. The tabs are the three legs. Everything below them — route, map, days — is only that leg.

Tap a circle to add or drop a stop. Miles, days and the itinerary recompute.

Tap a name for detail: what it costs, what it's like in late December, typical temperatures, and links out.

Map opens fitted to the leg. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a pin to open it. Names appear as you get closer.
