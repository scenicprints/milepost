Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org

Only the latin subsets are included, taken from the Google Fonts
API by tools/vendor-fonts.mjs. Re-run that tool to refresh them.

They are vendored rather than linked because Milepost has to work
with no signal, and the service worker ignores cross-origin
requests by design — so a linked font is a font you do not have
in the desert.
